# components/me

The Me tab as pure views (spec: `docs/specs/me.md`): `me-screen.tsx` (posts / year / gifts pills from the Me reference, then the grid), `year-select.tsx`, `sixty-five-grid.tsx` (3 columns, edge to edge; year 3×2, months 2×2 with the reference's gold frame, weeks 1×1, dense flow). Profile ornaments and bio from the reference are not built yet.

Filled tiles are buttons that open `tile-viewer.tsx` (photo and caption, read-only, ME-4).
