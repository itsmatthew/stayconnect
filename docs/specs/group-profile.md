# Group profile

A circle's own page: who's in it, what defines it, its recent photos, and the next time everyone will be together.
Reached from the group's Messages thread. Built from the designer's circle profile reference (phone, 390 wide); the wide layout keeps the same column, like the thread.
Left out of the reference on purpose: the superlatives, and any ranking of members (no "most active" aura, sparkles or labels); the gift shelf (the ornaments take its place); the settings gear.
Tests: `tests/group-profile/`.

| ID | Behaviour |
|----|-----------|
| GP-1 | **Open and close.** In a group thread (`docs/specs/messages.md`, MSG-3) the header logo and the name under it are both buttons, "Open ‹Group› profile". The profile replaces the thread, phone and wide, in the same column as the thread. "Back to the chat" returns to the thread. Friend threads have no profile button and their header is unchanged. |
| GP-2 | **Header.** The two header photos (GP-12) above the tree-colour band, the group's logo on the seam (`docs/specs/group-logo.md`, LOGO-2/3) and the name. A round "Change ‹Group› logo" button on the header opens the logo dialog (LOGO-4/5); it moved here from the thread. |
| GP-3 | **Members.** Everyone in the group, the user ("You") first, as avatars hanging from one string, each labelled with their name only: no ranking, aura or "most active" text anywhere. Tapping someone the user has placed in any ring of Your circle (`docs/specs/circle.md`) opens the user's 1:1 chat with them, as CIRCLE-6 does. Tapping anyone else does nothing yet. TODO(re-evaluate when the Me profile is built): tapping any member opens their profile. |
| GP-4 | **Ornaments.** The group's three ornaments (`docs/specs/home.md`, HOME-14), each its photo or art with its caption, are buttons "View ornament N of ‹Group›" (N = 1–3, in memory order). One opens a full-size view, "Ornament N of ‹Group›", with the caption, Close and "Edit". Edit opens the ornament sheet on that ornament (HOME-14–17). A change shows on the profile and on Home. |
| GP-5 | **Recent photos.** A 3-column grid, "Recent photos", of the 9 newest **visible** memories in the group chat, newest sent first. Sealed memories never appear (MSG-4). With more than 9, "See all" opens a full-screen grid, "All photos", of every visible memory, newest first, showing 24 and loading 24 more each time the end scrolls into view; "Back to the profile" closes it. With none: "No photos yet — send one from Daily 5". TODO(re-evaluate when the database exists): a "Together" view for photos captured on a past next-time-together date. |
| GP-6 | **Then.** A visible memory from this group whose capture date falls in the same ISO week as today (`docs/specs/archive.md`, ARC-2) in an earlier year. If several match, the oldest. A memory whose date is the file's modified date (`fileDated`, `docs/specs/daily5.md`, D5-4) never counts. It fills the left half of the next card (GP-7): its photo, "then", and "this week, N years ago" ("this week, 1 year ago"). With no match, the next card is full width. Sealed memories are never used. TODO(re-evaluate when the database exists): memories sent before `fileDated` was recorded carry no flag and are treated as capture-dated. |
| GP-7 | **Next time together.** Each group has at most one goal for the next time its members meet face to face: a label (required, trimmed, at most 32 characters) and an optional date. It is not a calendar. With no goal the card reads "Next time together" and a "Set a goal" button. With one, "next", then for a dated goal "‹N› days" ("1 day", "today") and "until ‹label›, ‹Mon D›"; for an undated goal, just the label. "Change goal" (or "Set a goal") opens a dialog, "Next time together": a "What's the plan?" text field (placeholder "lake weekend"), an optional "When" date field, Cancel and Save (enabled once the label isn't blank), and, when a goal exists, "Clear goal". Any member can set, change or clear it. |
| GP-8 | **I'm in.** Under the goal: the avatars of those who are in, "‹k› of ‹n› are in" (n is the members plus the user), and an "I'm in" button. Tapping it adds the user and it becomes "You're in · undo", which takes them out again. Setting a new goal, or changing its date (setting, moving or removing it), starts a fresh count with only the person who set it in. Changing only the label keeps everyone who's in. |
| GP-9 | **Past.** From the day after a dated goal's date, the goal is gone from the next card and from Home: the card shows "Set a goal" again and Home has no tag (`docs/specs/home.md`, HOME-19). The profile then asks how it went (GP-12) until someone answers. |
| GP-10 | **What everyone's up to.** Visual only: a "what everyone’s up to" section of rows, a date chip and a line of text, from the app's data. There is placeholder data only with the demo switch on. With no rows the section isn't shown. TODO(re-evaluate when members can share plans): real rows. |
| GP-12 | **Header photos.** The header's two halves are real photos, set by hand, on the profile header and the group's thread header (`docs/specs/messages.md`, MSG-3). **Left, "first photo together"**: the oldest photo of the group, where it started; under it "since ‹Mon YYYY›" from its capture date, or nothing when it has none. **Right, "last time together"**: a photo from the last time everyone was together; under it "‹Mon YYYY›" from its capture date, or nothing. It may stay unchanged for months. **Changing them:** only on the profile, by any member. Tapping a half picks any photo (a plain file picker, `accept="image/*"`, so the camera roll on phones), downscaled like ornament photos (JPEG, at most 1600 px), and saves it at once; its EXIF capture date is read on the way (`docs/specs/daily5.md`, D5-4). The pickers are labelled "Change first photo together" / "Change last time together". **Empty on the profile:** a dashed slot. Left: "Add your first photo together" and the helper "The oldest photo you can find of this group — the one where it started." Right: "Add a photo from the last time you were all together." **After a goal (GP-9):** while a dated goal is past, the right half on the profile is a prompt instead: "How was ‹label›? Add a photo from it". Picking one sets "last time together"; "Not now" dismisses it. Either way the past goal is cleared for good (TODO(re-evaluate when groups come from the database): "Not now" clears it for the whole group, since this browser is the group). Undated or future goals never prompt. **In the thread header** both halves are pictures only, named "‹Group› first photo together" and "‹Group› last time together", with their date line; an empty half shows the group's tree-colour art (its first and third ornaments' art), with no instructions. **Kept** with the ornaments and the logo (IndexedDB `nowandthen-ornaments`, slots `first` and `last`, with the demo suffix, DEMO-3). With the demo switch on, every placeholder group has both photos (drawn as art, with dates). With it off there are no groups (DEMO-2), so no header photos and no placeholder text; a real group starts with both halves empty. |
| GP-11 | **Kept, shared, demo.** Goals are kept in localStorage (`nowandthen.goals.v1`, with the demo suffix, `docs/specs/demo.md`, DEMO-3) and survive a reload. TODO(re-evaluate when groups come from the database): "any member" means this browser. With the demo switch on, Madrid Crew has the goal "lake weekend" 46 days from today with all 4 in, a few photos in its chat, and one memory from this week in a past year. With it off there are no groups, so no profile. |
