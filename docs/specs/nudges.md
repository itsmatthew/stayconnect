# Nudges & check-ins

Small, kind prompts to reach out, and a private Sunday question about your week. Built from the designer's UxNudges, UxReachOut and UxCheckIn references (phone, 390×844); wide screens keep the same sheets, centered. There is no server yet, so settings, answers and schedules stay in this browser, and nothing here can reach another person by itself (TODO(server)).
Tests: `tests/nudges/`.

A nudge never means anything about anyone: who comes up is only about time since you last talked, never a judgement of the friendship.

| ID | Behaviour |
|----|-----------|
| NUDGE-1 | **Settings.** Me has a "Nudges & check-ins" row that opens a full screen of that name, with "Back to Me". Sections in order, each a small-caps kicker with a rule: "Your check-in people", "Sunday check-in", "Reach-out nudges", "Quiet hours", then the 988 line (NUDGE-6). |
| NUDGE-2 | **Your check-in people.** "The people who hear when you choose to share a rough week. Pick the ones you’re closest to in real life, not just in the app." Then your friends as faces, closest ring first (nest, branches, bloom, then everyone else), each a toggle named by the friend with a red ring and a tick when picked. Under them: "N people picked. Two to four works best." ("1 person picked", or "Nobody picked yet. Two to four works best."). Nobody is picked at first, and you can unpick everyone. A last "by number" face ("Add someone not on the app") says "Adding someone by their number comes later." (TODO(server): texts). |
| NUDGE-3 | **Sunday check-in.** "Ask how my week went", "Sunday evenings. Only you see your answer.", a switch (on at first), and "Check in now", which opens the check-in (NUDGE-12) at once. |
| NUDGE-4 | **Reach-out nudges.** A three-way choice, "off", "1 a week" (at first) and "2 a week", with a line under it: off: "No nudges to reach out. Your Sunday check-in still comes."; 1: "One person a week who you haven’t caught up with, at a random time. A nudge never means anything about anyone."; 2: "Two a week, at random times. A nudge never means anything about anyone." |
| NUDGE-5 | **Quiet hours.** "11 pm to 9 am", "No nudges while you sleep.", a switch (on at first). While on, no nudge or check-in comes between 11 pm and 9 am local time; one due then waits until 9 am. |
| NUDGE-6 | **988.** "If it’s ever heavier than a rough week, you can call or text 988 anytime." 988 is a link that calls it (`tel:988`). It shows on the settings screen and on every check-in step after a rough week (NUDGE-13 to NUDGE-15). |
| NUDGE-7 | **Kept.** Settings are saved in this browser and survive a reload (TODO(server)). |
| NUDGE-8 | **When a reach-out nudge comes.** Each ISO week gets 1 or 2 times (NUDGE-4), on different days, seeded by the week so a reload never moves them. Each time is between 9 am and 11 pm when quiet hours are on, any hour when off. A nudge is due once its time has passed in the same week and it hasn't been shown; it shows on Circles (Home), once the intro, gift pop-ups and the tour are out of the way, and only one thing shows at a time (the check-in first). Missed weeks don't pile up. Off means none. Nothing shows to someone with no friends. |
| NUDGE-9 | **Reach out today.** A sheet over Home: "Reach out today", "Three people you haven’t caught up with in a while. They’ll be gladder than you think.", then up to three friends, longest since you last talked first (any message or memory either way in your 1:1 chat, sealed ones included; never talked counts as longest). Anyone you've talked with in the last 7 days is left out. Each row has their face, name, a line and one action: the first "Share a memory" ("You haven’t talked in N weeks", or "in a while" past 8 weeks, "You haven’t talked yet"); the second "Send a throwback" ("No memories shared since ‹Month›", or "No memories shared yet"); the third "Make plans" ("Haven’t caught up since ‹season›": winter, spring, summer, fall, or "in a while" past a year, "Haven’t caught up yet"). With fewer friends, fewer rows. Done rows show "Sent" instead of their action. "Close" (and Escape) puts it away for today; it doesn't come back until the next nudge. |
| NUDGE-10 | **Share a memory / Send a throwback.** A full screen: their face, a kicker ("SHARE A MEMORY" / "SEND A THROWBACK") and "with ‹name›"; "Pick one. These are photos you two share." Up to three memories you two share, as tiles labelled where they're from: "From your chat" (your 1:1 chat) or "From ‹circle›" (a group chat you're both in); for a throwback, oldest first, else newest first. Then "Choose from your photos" ("None of these? Your camera roll"), a file picker; the picked photo joins My photos (`docs/specs/daily5.md`, D5-2) and is picked. "NEED A START?": "remember when…", "still think about this", "we need to do this again", each adding its words to the note. A note field ("say something with it…"). "Send to ‹name›" waits for a photo, then sends it as a memory to your 1:1 chat with them, sealed until they reply like any memory (`docs/specs/messages.md`, MSG-4). "Back" returns to the sheet. |
| NUDGE-11 | **Sent.** "Sent to ‹name›", "Their ornament glows on your tree until they write back." (their face lit on Your circle, `docs/specs/circle.md`), the three faces with the done ones lit, "N of 3 today", and "Done", back to the sheet. |
| NUDGE-12 | **Make plans.** The same screen without photos: kicker "MAKE PLANS", starters "want to grab food this week?", "free this weekend?", "we should get something on the calendar", the note, and "Send to ‹name›", which sends the note as a text in your 1:1 chat (it waits for words). TODO(design): the reference only draws Share a memory. |
| NUDGE-13 | **Sunday check-in.** Due on Sundays from 6 pm local, once a week, while the switch is on (NUDGE-3, NUDGE-5); "Check in now" opens it any time. A sheet over Home: "SUNDAY CHECK-IN", "How have you really been this week?", "Not the “I’m fine” answer. There’s no wrong one.", five choices, Rough, Meh, Okay, Good and Great (each a drawn face, 44 to tap), and "Only you see your answer. Always." Your answer is kept only in this browser, with its week, and is never sent anywhere, even once there's a server. Rough goes to NUDGE-14. The others: "Thanks for checking in.", "See you next Sunday.", and "Close". TODO(design): only Rough is drawn. Close or Escape before answering skips this week. |
| NUDGE-14 | **A rough week.** "Thanks for being honest.", "Your people are here for you.", then "Let my people know I’m having a rough week" ("No shame in it. They’ll know to reach out.") and "Not right now" ("That’s okay too."), and the 988 line. |
| NUDGE-15 | **Letting them know.** With check-in people picked, it opens the device's share sheet (Web Share, else copying) with a ready text, "Having a rough week. Would mean a lot if you checked in on me.", so you send it yourself to whoever you choose; their faces and names show as "Send it to ‹names›", with "Send it" and "Not right now" (NUDGE-16). The app never says your people know unless a message really left (TODO(server): send it to them directly, then "Done. Your people know."). After a share: "Sent. Hope you have a better week. Remember, you are loved." With nobody picked: "Pick your check-in people first", which opens the settings. |
| NUDGE-16 | **Not right now.** "That’s okay.", "Nobody will know you answered. If you change your mind, you can check in anytime from Me.", the 988 line and "Close". |
| NUDGE-17 | **Kept.** Shown nudges, this week's check-in and its answer are saved in this browser, so a reload never shows them twice (TODO(server)). |
