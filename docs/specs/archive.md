# 65 Archive

65 photos a year per person: 52 weekly, 12 monthly and 1 yearly. You can't see your friends' photos for a week, month or year until you've posted your own for that same period.
Built from the designer's Archive feed reference (phone, 390×844). There is no database yet: your posts stay in this browser.
The Archive is **not** connected to Daily 5 or its photo pools: posting uses its own picker.
Tests: `tests/archive/`, `tests/app/`.

| ID | Behaviour |
|----|-----------|
| ARC-1 | **Nav.** The Archive nav button opens the Archive tab and Me opens the Me tab (`docs/specs/me.md`). Each becomes `aria-current="page"`. |
| ARC-2 | **Periods.** All in local time. **Weeks** run Monday–Sunday and are numbered the ISO way: a week belongs to the year its Thursday falls in, so Dec 29 2025 is in 2026's week 1. Weeks 1–52 are the weekly periods. **Week 53**, in years that have one (2026: Dec 28 – Jan 3), is the **bonus week · the week between years**, a period of its own with its own slot and look. It doesn't change the count: 65 = 52 + 12 + 1. **Months** are calendar months, the **year** is the calendar year. Period keys: `2026-W43`, `2026-B`, `2026-10`, `2026`. |
| ARC-3 | **One post per period, never replaced.** Weekly and bonus posts are open until Sunday 23:59. Monthly posts are open until the end of the month's last day. The yearly post is open only in December. Once you've posted, the slot offers no way to post again or replace it. When a period ends unposted, it closes for good. |
| ARC-4 | **Capture date.** The picker is a plain file input (`accept="image/*"`, so the camera roll on phones) and shows the allowed range at the top ("Photos taken Oct 19–25", "Photos taken Oct 1–31", "Photos taken in 2026"). Only the EXIF capture date counts (DateTimeOriginal, else CreateDate), never the file's modified date, which can be the upload time. A photo outside the period is rejected: "This photo is from Sep 12 — pick one from this week (Oct 19–25)." (month: "…from October (Oct 1–31).", year: "…from 2026.", bonus: "…from the bonus week (Dec 28 – Jan 3).") The photo's date carries its year when that differs from the period's ("Dec 30, 2025"). A photo with no capture date: "We can't tell when this photo was taken — pick one from this week (Oct 19–25)." *Not in this build:* in the native app, the picker will show a pre-filtered pool of only that period's photos. |
| ARC-5 | **The gate.** You see someone's post for a period only if you've posted your own for that same period, or you're a **viewer** on a circle they belong to (the grandma exception: viewers see that circle's members' posts without posting). Each period is gated separately. A missed period stays locked for good. |
| ARC-6 | **Audience.** Your posts go to everyone who shares at least one of your circles, not to a single group. The feed only holds posts from people who share a circle with you, never your own. |
| ARC-7 | **Circle lines and tags.** Each post card carries one very thin outline per circle you share with its author, in that circle's canopy colour, stacked outward: closest circles first, then in circle order, at most 3 lines. The card's colours come from the first of those circles, as in the reference. Tags are the shared circles' name pills, as in the reference: at most 2, then "+N". |
| ARC-8 | **Feed.** Below your slots: first the periods still open (week or bonus, then month, then year), then ended periods, most recently ended first, so a month's card follows its last week. The feed shows 8 periods at first; "Show earlier" adds 8 more each time. Weekly cards follow the reference. Monthly cards are bigger (taller photo), in a gold frame, with a "photo of October" ribbon. The yearly card is the biggest, in its own frame, "photo of 2026". Bonus-week cards have their own look: "Bonus week · the week between years". Placeholder posts draw the home screen's memory art in place of a photo. |
| ARC-9 | **Locked.** A period you haven't posted shows "N friends posted this week — post yours to see them" (1: "1 friend posted…"; month: "this month"; year: "this year"; bonus: "this bonus week") over blurred stand-in cards. The stand-ins are drawn in the authors' circle colours and never contain the photo. With nobody posted yet: "No one's posted this week yet — be the first". A missed, ended period reads "N friends posted week 41 — this one's closed" ("September", "2025", "the bonus week"). |
| ARC-10 | **Your slots**, at the top of the tab, each with clear directions. The **weekly** slot is the largest: "Post your photo of the week to see everyone's · closes Sunday". The **monthly** slot is small, "Photo of October · closes Oct 31", until the month's last 7 days, when it grows and reads "The photo that defines your October · closes Oct 31". The **yearly** slot only appears in December: "Your photo of 2026 · closes Dec 31". The **bonus** slot only appears in the bonus week: "Bonus week · the week between years · closes Sunday". After posting, a slot shows your photo with "unlocked ✓" and that period's feed opens below. |
| ARC-11 | **Explainer.** The first time the Archive opens, a one-time card reads "65 photos a year: 52 weeks, 12 months, 1 year. Post yours to see your friends'." with "Got it". Dismissed is remembered in localStorage. |
| ARC-12 | **Kept locally.** Your posts (metadata and a JPEG copy at most 1600 px on the long edge) live in the Archive's own IndexedDB (`nowandthen-archive`), separate from the Daily 5 database. Nothing is uploaded. Object URLs are revoked when the tab closes. |
| ARC-13 | **Caption.** Any post (weekly, monthly, yearly, bonus) can carry an optional one-line caption, typed in a text field under the photo preview before "Post": at most 80 characters, trimmed, and dropped if blank. Its placeholder fits the period: "what made this week", "what made the week between years", "what defined October", "what defined 2026". It shows under the photo on the feed card and when opening the tile in Me (ME-4). Like the photo, it can never be edited: once posted there is no field for it anywhere, and the store has no way to change a post. |

EXIF reading and image decoding need a real browser, so `captureDate` and `downscale` (`src/app/intro/photo-file.ts`) are checked by hand. The tests inject stand-ins and pin the rules around them. Frames, blur and sizes are visual and checked against the reference; the tests pin structure, labels, order and the gate.
