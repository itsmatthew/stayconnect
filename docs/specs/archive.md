# Archive

65 photos a year per person: 52 weekly, 12 monthly and 1 yearly. Everyone who shares a circle with you sees your posts in the Archive feed. "Post to see" (seeing a period's photos only once you've posted yours) is switched off for now and kept in the code behind one switch (ARC-5).
Built from the designer's "Archive — the feed" prototype (phone, 390×844), on the live design link. There is no database yet: your posts stay in this browser.
The Archive is **not** connected to Daily 5 or its photo pools: posting uses its own picker.
Tests: `tests/archive/`, `tests/app/`.

| ID | Behaviour |
|----|-----------|
| ARC-1 | **Nav.** The Archive nav button opens the Archive tab and Me opens the Me tab (`docs/specs/me.md`). Each becomes `aria-current="page"`. The tab's header, as designed: a bell (notifications, 1 new; does nothing yet), "Archive", and your face, which opens Me, over a double rule. On wide screens the bell and your face are in the top bar instead. |
| ARC-2 | **Periods.** All in local time. **Weeks** run Monday–Sunday and are numbered the ISO way: a week belongs to the year its Thursday falls in, so Dec 29 2025 is in 2026's week 1. Weeks 1–52 are the weekly periods. **Week 53**, in years that have one (2026: Dec 28 – Jan 3), is the **bonus week · the week between years**, a period of its own with its own slot and look. It doesn't change the count: 65 = 52 + 12 + 1. **Months** are calendar months, the **year** is the calendar year. Period keys: `2026-W43`, `2026-B`, `2026-10`, `2026`. |
| ARC-3 | **One post per period, never replaced.** Weekly and bonus posts are open until Sunday 23:59. Monthly posts are open until the end of the month's last day. The yearly post is open only in December. Once you've posted, the slot offers no way to post again or replace it. When a period ends unposted, it closes for good. |
| ARC-4 | **Capture date.** The picker is a plain file input (`accept="image/*"`, so the camera roll on phones) and shows the allowed range at the top ("Photos taken Oct 19–25", "Photos taken Oct 1–31", "Photos taken in 2026"). Only the EXIF capture date counts (DateTimeOriginal, else CreateDate), never the file's modified date, which can be the upload time. A photo outside the period is rejected: "This photo is from Sep 12 — pick one from this week (Oct 19–25)." (month: "…from October (Oct 1–31).", year: "…from 2026.", bonus: "…from the bonus week (Dec 28 – Jan 3).") The photo's date carries its year when that differs from the period's ("Dec 30, 2025"). A photo with no capture date: "We can't tell when this photo was taken — pick one from this week (Oct 19–25)." *Not in this build:* in the native app, the picker will show a pre-filtered pool of only that period's photos. |
| ARC-5 | **Who sees what.** You see every post from everyone who shares at least one circle with you, whether or not you've posted. "Post to see" is switched off for now: `POST_TO_SEE` in `src/app/intro/archive/gate.ts`. Switched on, you see someone's post for a period only if you've posted your own for that same period, or you're a **viewer** on a circle they belong to (the grandma exception); each period is gated separately and a missed one stays locked. TODO(re-evaluate when the Archive wants posting to unlock the feed again). |
| ARC-6 | **Audience.** Your posts go to everyone who shares at least one of your circles, not to a single group. The feed only holds posts from people who share a circle with you, never your own. |
| ARC-7 | **Who posted.** Above each photo: their face ringed in their first shared circle's color (closest circles first, then in circle order), their name, that circle's name after a dot in its color, them as a kid (TODO(server): their profile's "then" photo; their drawn face until then), and a send arrow (44 to tap) that opens a chat with them. |
| ARC-8 | **Feed.** Under this week's card, everyone's posts one after another, newest posted first, weeks, months and years mixed, never your own. 20 at first; "Show earlier" adds 20 more. Each photo is full width. A week's photo has the day it was taken and "WEEK 45" over its foot ("BONUS WEEK" for the bonus week); a month's or year's has a cream bar under it in the circle's color, "February · PHOTO OF THE MONTH" or "2025 · PHOTO OF THE YEAR". A band in the circle's color, fading to paper, parts the posts. With none: "Your circles' photos will show here." |
| ARC-9 | **Locked.** Off while "post to see" is off (ARC-5). The words for it are kept in `copy.ts` (`lockedText`): "N friends posted this week — post yours to see them" over stand-ins that never contain the photo. |
| ARC-10 | **This week's card**, at the top, as designed: "this week", the range ("Oct 19–25"), how long is left ("5 days left", "1 day left", "last day") and "closes Sunday night", the faces of friends who have posted this week (or the bonus week) with "2 of 5 friends have posted" ("1 of 5 friends has posted", "No one's posted yet this week"), out of everyone who shares a circle with you. Then a row (44 to tap) per photo you can add now: "Add your photo of the week · your circles are waiting on you"; "Add your photo of October · closes Oct 31" (in its last 7 days: "the photo that defines your October · closes Oct 31"); in December "Add your photo of 2026 · closes Dec 31"; in the bonus week, "Add your bonus week photo · the week between years · closes Sunday" in place of the week. Once yours is in, the row shows your photo and "Your photo of the week is in ✓", with no way to post again. |
| ARC-11 | **Explainer.** Retired: the design has no one-time "65 photos a year" card. |
| ARC-12 | **Kept locally.** Your posts (metadata and a JPEG copy at most 1600 px on the long edge) live in the Archive's own IndexedDB (`nowandthen-archive`), separate from the Daily 5 database. Nothing is uploaded. A post started before the app closes still finishes saving before the store closes. Object URLs are revoked when the tab closes. |
| ARC-13 | **Caption.** Any post (weekly, monthly, yearly, bonus) can carry an optional one-line caption, typed in a text field under the photo preview before "Post": at most 80 characters, trimmed, and dropped if blank. Its placeholder fits the period: "what made this week", "what made the week between years", "what defined October", "what defined 2026". It shows under the photo in the feed and when opening the tile in Me (ME-4). Like the photo, it can never be edited: once posted there is no field for it anywhere, and the store has no way to change a post. |

EXIF reading and image decoding need a real browser, so `captureDate` and `downscale` (`src/app/intro/photo-file.ts`) are checked by hand. The tests inject stand-ins and pin the rules around them. Frames, blur and sizes are visual and checked against the reference; the tests pin structure, labels, order and the gate.
