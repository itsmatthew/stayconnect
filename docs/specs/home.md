# Home — Your Circles

The home screen `/intro` crossfades into: the user's circles drawn as trees in a sunrise landscape.
Built from the designer's reference (phone, 390×844) and `docs/design/your-circles-desktop.png` (wide).
Tests: `tests/home/`.

| ID | Behaviour |
|----|-----------|
| HOME-1 | All placeholder data lives in one file (`src/app/intro/placeholder-data.ts`, marked TODO): groups, friends, seed conversations, placeholder replies and Archive posts. It loads only through the demo switch (`docs/specs/demo.md`). The Family circle has a viewer (Grandma, `docs/specs/archive.md`, ARC-5). Seven groups: the four **closest circles** first (Madrid Crew, Hoops Since ’14, Apt 4B, Family), then the three **other groups** (Uni Mates, Work Mates, Fall 24). Each group has an id, name, palette, `closest` flag, member friend ids, friend count, three memories (ornament art + caption) and a last-memory line. Users have 3–5 closest circles. |
| HOME-2 | Home opens centered on closest circle number `floor((n-1)/2)` (Hoops Since ’14 with four), so the center tree and its nearest neighbours are closest circles. Next moves the following group into the center, Previous the preceding one; rotation runs through the closest circles into the other groups and wraps around. Neighbours shown are always center ±1 on phones and center ±2 on wide screens, so the `closest` flag only sets the order: with fewer than five closest circles, the outer wide slots show the neighbouring groups in that order. |
| HOME-3 | Each visible group is a tree: canopy in its palette, shaded by one smooth radial gradient in its own colors (lighter top-left, slightly darker bottom-right) with one soft highlight, its three memories hung as ornaments, a name label and a friend-count badge. A soft halo ring glows around each canopy in that canopy's own color. The center tree is larger, its halo glows stronger, and it stands in a dotted ring of lights. The group trees are the only full-size trees; the scenery keeps just its small distant trees on the hills. |
| HOME-4 | The card shows the center group: its name on its color, a "swap photos" button that opens the ornament sheet (HOME-14) on its first ornament, "N FRIENDS", the three memories (thumbnail + caption) and the last-memory line, under a garland of lights. It updates on rotation and is announced politely (`aria-live`). |
| HOME-5 | The "your circles" / "your circle" toggle has exactly one option pressed, "your circles" initially. "your circle" switches to the private three-ring tree (`docs/specs/circle.md`, CIRCLE-1); "your circles" switches back. |
| HOME-6 | Header: the 365 logo (`public/logo-365.png`, as tall as the wordmark) then the "Now & Then" wordmark, Search, Notifications (1 new, red dot) and Your account (J avatar). Nav: Circles, Daily 5, Archive, Messages, Me, with Me shown as the person icon in both layouts (the J avatar appears only as Your account). Every nav item switches tabs and the current tab is `aria-current="page"` (`docs/specs/daily5.md`, D5-1; `docs/specs/archive.md`, ARC-1); Search, Notifications and Your account do nothing yet (TODO). |
| HOME-7 | Below 768px the layout matches the reference phone design (390×844), scaled uniformly to the viewport. The scenery is exactly `public/home-background.svg`, with its animations. Bottom nav, arrows at the lower left/right, card above the nav. |
| HOME-8 | At 768px and up the layout follows the desktop mockup: nav across the top (wordmark left, nav items centered, icons right), sitting directly on the sky with no bar, shadow or border behind it; the sky gradient reaches the top of the screen, toggle below it, centered on the screen's width, five slots in a row (center largest, see HOME-10), arrows at the row's sides, card centered at the bottom. The scenery widens to fill the screen: sky, ridges, ground and hills stretch; sun, clouds and town keep their shape. There is one church tower, centered behind the middle tree, and only the plain houses repeat. Its spire peeks out between the center tree's name and its canopy and never overlaps the name. Clouds are staggered at different heights and sizes. Small background trees spread across the width but stay clear of every group name label: none overlaps or sits behind label text. |
| HOME-9 | The fog drifts, the river flows (phone) and the ring of lights pulses; all of it stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Decorative SVG is `aria-hidden`; the arrows are labelled "Previous circle" and "Next circle". |
| HOME-10 | Wide row: always five trees in the mockup's five slots (216 / 431 / 656 / 881 / 1096 in the 1312-wide canvas, centered on the screen), the center tree largest, at the screen's horizontal center, with the dotted ring and the card directly under it. |
| HOME-11 | **No river on wide screens.** At 768px and up neither the scenery nor the tree row draws a river, and the small trees keep clear of the labels only. Below 768px the phone scenery keeps its river unchanged (HOME-7). |
| HOME-12 | **No circles yet.** With no groups, home shows a single young sapling on the cream paper, "Your circles grow when someone sends you a memory — or send one first", and a "Go to Daily 5" button that opens Daily 5 (`docs/specs/demo.md`, DEMO-2). The toggle sits above it, so "your circle" is reachable with no circles (CIRCLE-11). TODO: replaced by the new-user home in `docs/specs/memory-invites.md`. |
| HOME-13 | **Ornaments are buttons.** Every ornament on every visible tree, in both layouts, is a button "Change ornament N of ‹group›" (N = 1–3, in memory order). Tapping one opens the ornament sheet on that ornament. |
| HOME-14 | **Ornament sheet.** Ornaments are the group's defining memories. A dialog titled "‹Group›’s defining memories". It shows the group's three ornaments as a choice (the open one pressed), then the selected one's current photo and caption. "Choose a photo" is a plain file picker (`accept="image/*"`, so the camera roll on phones): any photo, with no date or group restriction. Under it, the helper text "Pick a photo that defines this group — the inside joke, the trip, the night everyone still talks about." A picked photo opens in the round cropper (`docs/specs/group-logo.md`, LOGO-7) to position and zoom it. The caption is optional (placeholder "what makes this one ours"): one line, at most 40 characters, prefilled with the current caption, trimmed, and blank means none. **Save** is enabled once a photo is picked or the caption changed. Cancel, the close button or the backdrop discards the edit. Picked photos are downscaled like Archive photos (JPEG, at most 1600 px on the long edge), and the saved ornament is the cropped square (JPEG, at most 1024 px). |
| HOME-15 | **Shared by the group.** Any member can change any of the group's ornaments, and the change replaces that ornament on the tree and on the card for everyone in the group. TODO(re-evaluate when ornaments come from the database): there is no server yet, so changes live in this browser (IndexedDB `nowandthen-ornaments`) and only the user makes them. |
| HOME-16 | **Who changed it.** Under the selected ornament the sheet reads "‹Name› changed this · Oct 21" ("You changed this · Oct 21" for the user; with the year when it isn't the current one: "Oct 21, 2025"). An ornament nobody has changed shows no line. |
| HOME-17 | **Undo.** Each ornament keeps the photo, caption and attribution it had before its last change. When there is one, the sheet shows "undo", and any member can tap it to restore that version. One level only: after an undo there is nothing to undo until the next change. Images that can no longer be restored are deleted. |
| HOME-18 | **Demo switch.** Ornament storage gets the `.demo` suffix with the switch on (DEMO-3). With it off there are no groups, so nothing to change. No placeholder photos: the picker is only the file picker, in both modes. TODO(re-evaluate when chats come from the database): add a "from the chat" row so members can pick a photo someone else already shared in the group. |

Pixel layout, the wide-scenery stretch and the animations are visual: jsdom can't lay out or animate. The tests pin the structure: layers, `preserveAspectRatio`, animation classes, and the phone scenery matching `home-background.svg` element for element. Fidelity is checked against the screenshots in `docs/design/`.
