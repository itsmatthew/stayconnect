# Home — Your Circles

The home screen `/intro` crossfades into: the user's circles drawn as trees in a sunrise landscape.
Built from the designer's reference (phone, 390×844) and `docs/design/your-circles-desktop.png` (wide).
Tests: `tests/home/`.

| ID | Behaviour |
|----|-----------|
| HOME-1 | All placeholder data lives in one file (`src/app/intro/placeholder-data.ts`, marked TODO): groups, friends, seed conversations and placeholder replies. Seven groups: the four **closest circles** first (Madrid Crew, Hoops Since ’14, Apt 4B, Family), then the three **other groups** (Uni Mates, Work Mates, Fall 24). Each group has an id, name, palette, `closest` flag, member friend ids, friend count, three memories (ornament art + caption) and a last-memory line. Users have 3–5 closest circles. |
| HOME-2 | Home opens centered on closest circle number `floor((n-1)/2)` (Hoops Since ’14 with four), so the center tree and its nearest neighbours are closest circles. Next moves the following group into the center, Previous the preceding one; rotation runs through the closest circles into the other groups and wraps around. Neighbours shown are always center ±1 on phones and center ±2 on wide screens, so the `closest` flag only sets the order: with fewer than five closest circles, the outer wide slots show the neighbouring groups in that order. |
| HOME-3 | Each visible group is a tree: canopy in its palette, shaded by one smooth radial gradient in its own colors (lighter top-left, slightly darker bottom-right) with one soft highlight, its three memories hung as ornaments, a name label and a friend-count badge. A soft halo ring glows around each canopy in that canopy's own color. The center tree is larger, its halo glows stronger, and it stands in a dotted ring of lights. The group trees are the only full-size trees; the scenery keeps just its small distant trees on the hills. |
| HOME-4 | The card shows the center group: its name on its color, "swap photos", "N FRIENDS", the three memories (thumbnail + caption) and the last-memory line, under a garland of lights. It updates on rotation and is announced politely (`aria-live`). |
| HOME-5 | The "your circles" / "your circle" toggle has exactly one option pressed, "your circles" initially. Content doesn't change yet (TODO until the "your circle" view is designed). |
| HOME-6 | Header: the 365 logo (`public/logo-365.png`, as tall as the wordmark) then the "Now & Then" wordmark, Search, Notifications (1 new, red dot) and Your account (J avatar). Nav: Circles, Daily 5, Archive, Messages, Me, with Me shown as the person icon in both layouts (the J avatar appears only as Your account). Circles, Daily 5 and Messages switch tabs and the current tab is `aria-current="page"` (`docs/specs/daily5.md`, D5-1); Search, Notifications, Your account, Archive and Me do nothing yet (TODO). |
| HOME-7 | Below 768px the layout matches the reference phone design (390×844), scaled uniformly to the viewport. The scenery is exactly `public/home-background.svg`, with its animations. Bottom nav, arrows at the lower left/right, card above the nav. |
| HOME-8 | At 768px and up the layout follows the desktop mockup: nav across the top (wordmark left, nav items centered, icons right), sitting directly on the sky with no bar, shadow or border behind it; the sky gradient reaches the top of the screen, toggle below it, centered on the screen's width, five slots in a row (center largest, see HOME-10), arrows at the row's sides, card centered at the bottom. The scenery widens to fill the screen: sky, ridges, ground and hills stretch; sun, clouds, town and river keep their shape. There is one church tower, centered behind the middle tree, and only the plain houses repeat. Its spire peeks out between the center tree's name and its canopy and never overlaps the name. Clouds are staggered at different heights and sizes. Small background trees spread across the width but stay clear of every group name label and of the river: none overlaps or sits behind label text, and the river never runs over one. |
| HOME-9 | The fog drifts, the river flows and the ring of lights pulses; all of it stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Decorative SVG is `aria-hidden`; the arrows are labelled "Previous circle" and "Next circle". |
| HOME-10 | Wide row: always five trees in the mockup's five slots (216 / 431 / 656 / 881 / 1096 in the 1312-wide canvas, centered on the screen), the center tree largest, at the screen's horizontal center, with the dotted ring and the card directly under it. |
| HOME-11 | Wide river: one solid colour from top to bottom (the phone river's blue), outlined like the phone river. It rises at the horizon beside the sun, passes behind the center tree's canopy, comes out at its lower right and winds down through the gap between the center tree and its right neighbour, then ends behind the card. Apart from passing behind the center canopy it never touches any tree's canopy or trunk, or the center tree's friend-count badge. Its path is computed from the center tree's position and size, not from fixed screen coordinates. The phone scenery keeps its own river unchanged. |

Pixel layout, the wide-scenery stretch and the animations are visual: jsdom can't lay out or animate. The tests pin the structure: layers, `preserveAspectRatio`, animation classes, and the phone scenery matching `home-background.svg` element for element. Fidelity is checked against the screenshots in `docs/design/`.
