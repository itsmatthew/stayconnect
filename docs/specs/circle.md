# Your circle

The private view behind Home's "your circle" toggle: one big tree with three rings, bloom (outer), branches (middle) and nest (center), and the people the user has placed in each.
Built from the designer's "your circle" reference (phone, 390×844); the wide layout keeps the same tree and card side by side.
Tests: `tests/circle/`.

| ID | Behaviour |
|----|-----------|
| CIRCLE-1 | **The view.** "your circle" on the toggle (phone and wide, and on the empty home) swaps the group trees, arrows and group card for one big tree in the same scenery, with three canopy rings labelled bloom (outer), branches and nest (center). Each placed person's avatar sits in their ring. "your circles" brings the group view back. |
| CIRCLE-2 | **Soft guides, never limits.** Nest is about 5 (roughly 0–10), branches about 15 (roughly 5–25), bloom about 50 (roughly 25–75). Any count is allowed. Above the upper end, the card shows a gentle note for that ring: "Your nest is getting big — most people keep it under 10", "Your branches are getting full — most people keep them under 25", "Your bloom is getting big — most people keep it under 75". |
| CIRCLE-3 | **Balanced at any count.** Avatars are spread evenly and symmetrically about the trunk within their ring's band. They never overlap each other or the ring labels, and never leave the band. As a ring fills, its avatars stay as large as they can: first they fill more rows, then they shrink. A single nest avatar sits at the center. Past what fits at the smallest size, the last spot is a "+N" chip for the rest. |
| CIRCLE-4 | **Lit or dim.** An avatar is lit (colour, glow, twinkle) when the user and that person exchanged a message or memory in their 1:1 chat in the last 30 days, counting memories the user sent that are still sealed. Otherwise it is dim (greyscale). The twinkle stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`. |
| CIRCLE-5 | **The card.** It has Nest / Branches / Bloom tabs with counts (Nest is selected first) and a one-line blurb for the selected ring: "your home people, the ones you love most", "the friends you keep close", "the wider circle you're glad to know". Below that, the ring's people (scrolling sideways when long) and an "add" button. Under everything: "Only you see your circle. No one is told which ring they're in." |
| CIRCLE-6 | **Tap to talk.** Tapping an avatar in the tree opens the user's chat thread with that person in Messages. If they've never talked, the thread opens empty. |
| CIRCLE-7 | **Drag to move.** Press-and-hold an avatar in the tree picks the person up. While dragging, the ring under the pointer is highlighted. Dropping on another ring moves them there and briefly announces "Leo moved to Nest". Dropping on their own ring, or outside the tree, changes nothing. |
| CIRCLE-8 | **Move from the card.** Tapping a person in the card opens a menu: "Message Leo", then "Move to …" for the two other rings. "add" opens a picker of everyone not already in that ring; people in another ring show which one. Picking someone places them, or moves them in from their ring. No ring ever blocks an add. |
| CIRCLE-9 | **Suggested moves, inward only.** Someone in branches or bloom with enough 1:1 activity in the last 30 days gets a suggestion. Activity means messages and memories, and both sides must have sent something. Moving into nest takes 6 exchanges; moving into branches takes 3. The suggestion reads "You and Leo have been close lately — move to Nest?" (or "…move to Branches?"). Only one shows at a time, most active first. "Move" moves them; "Not now" dismisses it, and that person isn't suggested again for 30 days. Nothing moves without the user's approval. There are no outward suggestions, and nest members are never suggested. Group chats don't count. |
| CIRCLE-10 | **Kept.** Placements and dismissed suggestions are kept in localStorage (with the demo suffix, `docs/specs/demo.md`, DEMO-3) and survive a reload. New users start with empty rings. |
| CIRCLE-11 | **Empty and demo.** With the demo switch off, the tree is empty (rings and labels, no avatars, no gifts), the card counts are 0 and it says "Your circle fills as you connect with people". With the switch on, the placeholder friends are placed by `placeholder-data.ts`. |
| CIRCLE-12 | **Gift pile.** Only gifts the user has actually received sit under the tree; there are none by default, and with none there's just the trunk. Each gift is one wrapped box standing on the ground at the trunk's base (bottoms on the ground line, each with a soft shadow), beside the trunk and never over it. Boxes alternate left and right, the newest nearest the trunk, each next one a little further out and slightly overlapping its neighbour, the newer in front. Up to 4 boxes show, then a "+N" chip on the ground counts the rest. The pile is announced as "1 gift received" / "N gifts received". With the demo switch on there are 2 placeholder gifts. Opening a gift does nothing yet. TODO: this is where the memory mailbox goes. |

Placement is the user's call alone: nothing in the app moves anyone without an explicit drag, menu choice, add or accepted suggestion.
Pixel layout, drag feel and the glow/twinkle are visual, so jsdom can't check them; they're compared against the designer's reference. The tests pin the geometry (`ring-layout.ts`), the rules (`rings.ts`, `suggestions.ts`) and the wiring.
