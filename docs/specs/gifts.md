# Gifts

A paid way to send one framed photo and a handwritten postcard to one person or one group. Built from `design-ref/project/GiftCompose.dc.html` and `MeGifts.dc.html` (phone, 390×844); wider screens extend the phone design.
The rules are plain TypeScript in `src/app/intro/gifts/`, so the mobile app can import them unchanged.
TODO(server): gifts are saved in this browser. Nothing reaches another person yet.
Tests: `tests/gifts/`.

Phase 1 is the gift maker. Checkout, unwrapping, the shelf and the mailbox come next.

Pulling it eases the drawer down (.25s), then the gift maker slides up over the shelf (.6s) with its top corners squaring off. "Cancel and go back to your gifts" slides it back down. | GIFT-2 | **Five steps.** To, Photo, Frame, Note, Wrap, as chips: the number (or ✓ once done) then the name. The current chip is navy, finished ones are paper with a navy border, the rest are clear. "Next: ‹step›" moves on only when the step is complete; Back keeps everything. A chip opens any step up to the first unfinished one. The step's panel scrolls inside itself, so Back and Next stay on screen. Tap targets are at least 44px, except inks and stamps (GIFT-7) |
| GIFT-3 | **Live card.** One 296×226 card on a warm-glow stage, bobbing gently: the framed photo on the front, the postcard on the back, and the wrapping laid over it on Wrap. It shows the postcard on To and Note, the photo on Photo and Frame. "turn it over" and "see the photo" flip it (.75s, a slight overshoot). Under reduced motion nothing bobs, flips or slides |
| GIFT-4 | **To.** "Who's it for?" lists "your groups" first, each with its logo in the color and name this viewer sees, then "your nest" and "branches" from Your circle, then an "everyone" button for the rest. A group with nobody else in it isn't offered. Search covers groups and everyone. With nobody to pick, it says so. Picking sets the title to "A gift for ‹name›". |
| GIFT-5 | **Photo.** The newest photos from Daily 5's pools, 30 at a time with "Show more". With no photos, it says so and offers Daily 5. |
| GIFT-6 | **Frame.** Classic ("clean white border"), Instant ("room to write the date"), Album ("tucked into photo corners"), Film ("one frame off the roll"), Wood ("framed like it’s on a shelf"), and a month frame ("changes with the month") named for the month it's sent in, with falling leaves and "November '26". The "Date stamp" toggle prints the capture date in orange dot-matrix digits ("11 05 '26"). A photo dated only by its file can't have one (`docs/specs/daily5.md`, D5-4), so the toggle is off and disabled, with a line saying why |
| GIFT-7 | **Note.** "Write on the back": up to 140 characters, with a counter, cut off at the limit, and an emoji counts as one. The placeholder, also shown faintly on an empty postcard, is "Something only ‹name› would get…". It writes onto the postcard as you type, in Pen, Pencil or Script handwriting and Navy, Red, Green or Graphite ink, with a Tree, Sunrise, 365 or Heart stamp. Inks and stamps share one row, as designed: 40px wide, at least 44 tall. The postcard is signed "— ‹sender›", postmarked with today's date, and addressed "‹name› / under their tree" |
| GIFT-8 | **Wrap.** Candy stripe, Gold dots, Kraft, Gingham, Newsprint or Night sky paper, a Gold, Red, Cream or Navy ribbon, and "right away" or "on a day" (then "Pick a day", which opens the date picker). A chosen day must be after today. On reaching Wrap, two torn halves of paper slide in, the ribbons run along and across, the bow pops on and the tag swings in; changing the paper doesn't replay it. The tag reads "to ‹name›", with "open ‹date›" (or "open later") for a chosen day. The button is "Send to ‹name›". TODO(re-evaluate when checkout is built): it's off until then |
| GIFT-9 | **Never lost.** From the moment a person is picked, the gift is saved after every change, under `nowandthen.gifts.v1` plus the demo suffix (`docs/specs/demo.md`, DEMO-3). Closing the maker keeps it, and "send a gift" picks it up on the same step. Saving goes through `GiftStore`, whose methods are async. |
| GIFT-10 | **Catalog.** A gift stores ids for its frame, handwriting, ink, stamp, wrap and ribbon, never their looks. Every catalog item says whether it's free or paid. All are free for now. |
| GIFT-11 | **Analysis.** Gifts made and abandoned are recorded through `GiftEvents`, under `nowandthen.giftEvents.v1` plus the demo suffix. A gift is made when a person is first picked, and abandoned when the maker closes with it unsent. Our own data only, nothing third-party. Never shown to anyone in the app. |
| GIFT-12 | **Portable.** The logic files in `src/app/intro/gifts/` use no React or browser APIs. Only the localStorage adapters, the hook and the client may. |
| GIFT-13 | **Group gifts.** A gift can go to a group. It costs one price in total, not one per member: one charge per gift, whoever it's for. The tag reads "to ‹group name›", and the postcard is addressed to the group. Each member gets their own copy, with its own id, to unwrap and put on their own shelf. The sender gets no copy. Who gets a copy is decided when the gift is sent, so someone joining later gets none. |

The design draws papers, sprockets and perforations with CSS repeating gradients, tears and tags with clip paths, and shadows with filters: none port to React Native. Here they're SVG patterns, polygons and drawn shadows, with plain linear gradients and transforms, so the look matches and ports. A test keeps CSS filters, blend modes, backdrop blur and repeating gradients out. The look is checked by hand against the design's numbers; the tests pin structure.
