# Onboarding

How a brand-new person meets the app, from the designer's "New user, downloaded on their own" (WelcomeLetter.dc.html) and "New user, invited by a friend" (InviteReceive.dc.html) prototypes (phone, 390×844), on the live design link.
There is no database yet: everything here stays in this browser, like the mailbox it lives in (`docs/specs/mailbox.md`).
Tests: `tests/onboarding/`.

| ID | Behaviour |
|----|-----------|
| ONB-1 | **The welcome letter arrives.** Someone with no circles gets one welcome letter in their mailbox the first time the app opens, as new mail. It arrives once: reading it, or later having circles, never brings it back. With demo data (`docs/specs/demo.md`) there are circles, so no letter. Its day is the day it arrived. Saved with the mailbox (`nowandthen.inbox.v1`, MAILBOX-10). |
| ONB-2 | **Start here.** While the letter is new and you have no circles, Home shows a callout under the toggle, "Start here · Tap **your circle** to see your tree.", over the empty home (HOME-12). On your circle, a callout by the mailbox reads "You’ve got mail · Tap your mailbox to open it.", with a pulsing ring around the mailbox. Motion is off under reduced motion. |
| ONB-3 | **On your tree.** The letter counts as new mail: the flag is up and the badge counts it with any photos and birthdays, and the mailbox reads "Your mailbox, a welcome letter" ("…, a welcome letter and 2 new photos", "…, a welcome letter and 1 birthday coming up"). |
| ONB-4 | **The envelope.** Tapping the mailbox while the letter is new opens it first, before birthdays and photos: over your tree, dimmed, "Your mailbox" and "1 new", and one cream envelope that bobs: the Now & Then mark with "Now & Then" and "Harrison & Jackson" as the return address, a "N&T FOREVER" tree stamp, a round "GAINESVILLE FL" postmark with the letter's day ("NOV 8", "2026") and wavy bars, your name in handwriting (TODO(server): your profile's name, from `GET /api/me`; "you" until then), and "tap to open". Tapping it ("Open the welcome letter") lifts the flap and slides the letter up. × closes it, still new. |
| ONB-5 | **The letter.** A lined page with a double rule under "welcome to · Now & Then" and the letter's day ("Sun, Nov 8", "2026"), "Hey ‹your name›," ("Hey there," with no name yet), the two opening paragraphs, the five features as designed (65 Archive, Daily 5, Your circles, Your circle, Gifts), each with its icon and two lines, "100% private. No ads, ever. We will never sell your data.", "Invite your people and spread the word.", and "Glad you’re here, Harrison & Jackson" in handwriting. It scrolls under a fade at the top. Under it, "Start my first circle" (46 tall, ONB-7). × ("Close the letter") closes it. |
| ONB-6 | **Read, then kept.** Opening the letter reads it: closing it after that, or starting a circle, keeps it with your mail (MAILBOX-5) under the week or month it arrived, as a tile, "Welcome letter · from Harrison & Jackson", before that group's photos. Tapping the tile opens the letter again. |
| ONB-7 | **Start my first circle.** Under the letter, "Start my first circle" closes the mailbox (the letter is kept, ONB-6) and opens "Start a circle" over the app, on a cream page. Its top row: a back arrow ("Back to the letter" on step 1, which reopens the letter; "Back" after) and four dots, the current one long and in the circle's color ("Step 2 of 4"). Back keeps what you'd entered. Steps 1 to 4 end with a 58-tall button at the foot. |
| ONB-8 | **Step 1: name and color.** "Start a circle", "Your crew from home, the roommates, the team. Give it a name.", a "Circle name" field (placeholder "e.g. Red Robin", at most 22 characters) and "Pick its color": the twelve group colors (`docs/specs/group-colors.md`, COLOR-1) as 48-wide round swatches, a radio group ("Circle color"), five across. The suggested color (COLOR-3) starts picked. When one of your circles already has the picked color, the swatch shows the shade yours will get and a note says "‹Circle› already uses red, so yours gets its own shade." (COLOR-6). "Next" is grey and does nothing until the name has a letter or digit; then it's in the circle's color. |
| ONB-9 | **Step 2: logo.** "Pick a logo", "Made from your name. You can upload your own later in the circle’s profile.", the logo big (150), the name under it, and three styles in a radio group, "Logo style": **Crest** (the name's first letter, slanted, with an offset shadow in the circle's color, on navy with a star and a smile), **Badge** (the monogram in italic cream on the circle's color, "EST · ‹year›") and **Varsity** (the monogram in block capitals, outlined, on cream with a thick ring and three stars). The monogram is the first letters of the first two words, or the first two characters of a one-word name, upper-cased ("Red Robin" → RR, "Family" → FA). Crest starts picked. |
| ONB-10 | **Step 3: now & then.** "Now & then", "One of you all now, one from back in the day. They sit at the top of your chat.": two halves side by side, "Now" and "Then", each "tap to add" (a plain file picker, `accept="image/*"`), the logo on their seam, the name, and "You can change these anytime". A picked photo fills its half (tap again to pick another). The button says "Skip for now" until one is picked, then "Next". They become the circle's header photos, "Then" its first photo together and "Now" its last time together (`docs/specs/group-profile.md`, GP-12). |
| ONB-11 | **Step 4: your invite.** "Your invite" and the invite card as your friends will see it: the logo, the name, "a circle on Now & Then", "from ‹your name›" in handwriting, the circle's tree on a hill with three empty ornament spots, your face and a glowing dashed "you" spot, "Your spot’s saved.", "‹your name› started ‹Circle› and saved you a spot.", a "Take your spot" button drawn in the circle's color, and "nowandthen.app". Under it, "sent with it" and the message in a blue bubble: "saved you a spot on our tree 🌲 come join ‹Circle›". "Share invite" opens your phone's share sheet with that message and the circle's invite link (MAIL-5's share). Shared or copied, the circle is made and step 5 follows; cancelled, you stay here. TODO(server): the link carries the circle's name and color itself until invites live on the server (issue #26). |
| ONB-12 | **Live.** The logo drops in with a burst of confetti in the circle's colors, "‹Circle› is live" and "Your people are on their way." ("Link copied. Paste it in your group chat." when it was copied), and "Go to my circle", which closes it on Home, on the new circle. Motion is off under reduced motion. |
| ONB-13 | **Your new circle.** It's on Home, in Messages and wherever groups are, from the moment it's made: its name, its color (at the shade it got, COLOR-6), the logo you picked as its logo (LOGO-4; replace it any time from the profile), your photos as its header photos, and you as its only member until people join. Saved in this browser (`nowandthen.circles.v1`, with the demo suffix, DEMO-3). TODO(server): `POST /api/groups` makes it for real. |
